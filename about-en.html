About Us

Welcome to Discover Germany. This website was created to provide clear and easy-to-understand information about Germany and its 16 federal states.

Germany is a diverse country with different landscapes, cities, cultures, historical places and regional characteristics. Our goal is to present this diversity in a simple and well-organized way.

On our website, you can find information about all 16 federal states. This includes geographical location, major cities, well-known landmarks, nature, history and other interesting characteristics of each region.

Our Goal

Our goal is to create a simple source of information for people who want to learn more about Germany. The content is intended to provide a quick overview while also encouraging visitors to discover more about Germany's individual regions.

The website is continuously being developed and may be expanded with additional information and content in the future.

Information and Accuracy

Most of the content on this website was created with the assistance of artificial intelligence, with the aim of providing clear and helpful information. Nevertheless, some information may contain errors or inaccuracies or may become outdated over time. We are continuously working to improve and update the content and welcome reports that help us correct information and further improve the website.

If you find incorrect or inaccurate information, we would appreciate it if you reported it to us. This allows us to review the relevant content and improve it where appropriate.
