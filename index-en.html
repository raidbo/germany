Germany

Germany is located in Central Europe and consists of 16 federal states. The country offers a great diversity of landscapes, cities, cultures and historical landmarks. From the North Sea and Baltic Sea coasts through forests, rivers and low mountain ranges to the Alps in the south, there are numerous different regions to discover.
